## Summary
Recent graduates can do an Erasmus+ traineeship if their university selects them during their last year of study and they complete the traineeship within 12 months of graduating. The traineeship counts towards the 12-month maximum of the study cycle in which they applied.

## Details
- Selection happens while you are still a student (final year), not after graduation.
- The traineeship must be completed within 12 months after graduating.
- Its duration counts towards the 12-month maximum of that study cycle (see erasmus-duration-rules).
