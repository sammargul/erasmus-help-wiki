## Summary
Students and recent graduates with fewer opportunities receive an extra monthly amount on top of the normal Erasmus+ grant, if they meet the criteria set by their country's national agency.

## Details
- The top-up is added to the monthly individual support.
- Who counts as "fewer opportunities" is defined at national level, not by the EU alone.
- One institution reports a top-up of 250 EUR per month for long-term mobilities (2-12 months).
