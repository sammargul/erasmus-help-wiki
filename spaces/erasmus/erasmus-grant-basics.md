## Summary
The Erasmus+ grant is a monthly contribution towards the extra cost of living abroad, not a full salary or scholarship. How much you get depends mainly on the country you go to and on rates set by your home country's national agency, so always confirm the exact amount with your home university's International Office.

## Details
- The grant is paid per month of your mobility period (individual support).
- Host countries are grouped by cost of living; more expensive destinations get a higher monthly rate.
- The exact monthly rates are fixed by the national agency of your home country, so students from different countries can receive different amounts for the same destination.
- Students with fewer opportunities receive an extra monthly top-up when they meet the criteria set at national level (see erasmus-fewer-opportunities-top-up).
- Separate travel support can be paid on top of the monthly grant, with extra support for green travel.
