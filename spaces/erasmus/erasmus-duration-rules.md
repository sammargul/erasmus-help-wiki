## Summary
A long-term Erasmus+ stay lasts between 2 and 12 months. Short-term stays of 5 to 30 days must be blended (combined with an online part). In total, one student can spend at most 12 months abroad with Erasmus+ per study cycle (for example per Bachelor's or per Master's), split over one or more stays.

## Details
- Long-term mobility: minimum 2 months, maximum 12 months abroad (blended mobilities with a virtual component included).
- Short-term mobility: between 5 and 30 days abroad; these must be blended mobilities.
- Maximum total: 12 months within one study cycle; more than one exchange is possible within that limit.
- Only doctoral candidates can do a short-term mobility without a virtual component.

## Open questions
- None for the general rules. Your home university may set stricter internal limits (for example on funded months).
