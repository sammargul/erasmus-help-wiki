## Summary
You apply through your own (home) university, not directly to the EU. Start by contacting your home university's International Office, which selects students, nominates them to the partner, and helps prepare the learning agreement.

## Details
- Contact your home university's International Office or Erasmus+ coordinator first.
- Your home university selects and nominates you for a place at a partner university or traineeship host.
- Studies and traineeships are approved through a learning agreement (for traineeships: a traineeship learning agreement).
